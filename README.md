Celestial Voyage Planet Explorer 

Choose the Moon, Mars, or Venus, build a spacecraft that fits your mission requirements, and make choices during your voyage to collect science and reach your destination.

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Within the game, player can build a spacecraft to explore Space
Three destinations: The Moon, Mars, and Venus, each with levels of difficulty.
Spacecraft builder: Pick engines, solar panels, shields, antennas and scientific instruments.
Space adventures: Encounter asteroids, solar flares, communication problems and scientific discoveries.
Mission results: Earn points, complete missions, or redesign your spacecraft after a failed attempt.

How to play
1. celestialvoyage.netlify.app
2. Open the link attached on the GitHub repository.
3. Choose a planet and start building your spacecraft.
4. Launch your mission
   
How to run the code
1. Download all the files attached 
2. Open on the files within Visual Studio Code
